About

A place for the search.

This is a collection of my work and an expression of my search for meaning and my place in this existence.

The name comes from the first online avatar I created in 2002. Though he—or it—has evolved significantly, sgttomas is nevertheless the way I channel myself through this medium.

There is no canonical way to pronounce this name. If “sergeant” is used, there is no intention to imply that I have at any time been in the military or police. My heartfelt respect goes to those men and women who serve honorably.

Please let me know what you find in it. Peace.
