Inspirations

Ryan Tufts · December 18, 2021

1 min read

The connection formed when two people come together with the intention to be present with one another and truly seek the pleasure of that encounter is the sweet nectar of life. This post is in honor of a young man and an inspiring artist who brought out of me the yearning to express myself through the mediums on display here. Thank you. I drew this image in his honor. I’m at my best when I can disappear into that shared space and seek the beauty of the moment and the splendour of God’s Names. We all have this light, this universe, this splendorous beauty inside of us. I do believe that despite what we think we see and knowing that sin is real. It’s real. I chose light.
