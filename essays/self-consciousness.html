Dialogue · December 18, 2021

Self consciousness

So it may be the very self consciousness that inspires art also hides it.

My desire to share the meaning of life’s experience with you is too intense to be denied though. Please forgive my shortcomings and please point out my inconsistencies.

I hope that this website adds something beautiful to the shared human experience and this wondrous journey into the unknown beyond. And the One who brought us here will take us back again.

The Meaning Project is a labour of love and of necessity that I have been working on for almost two decades.

We don’t have a direct experience of God in this life, but there are signs. His Names bring forth existence with the mere “be” and it is.
