Dialogue · June 19, 2020

Tell me what you think

If you have a response to my work I would be grateful to hear from you.

The content here didn’t arise from some plan but came over time in response to my circumstances and dispositions. I have put this out in the world now for people to engage with, that it might be a new thing for them.

Meaning arises within us without effort and seemingly without any intention on our part. We can certainly shape meanings, but the genesis of life as something meaningful has astounded me without ceasing.

This is some of what I found meaningful.

Yours truly,
~sgttomas
