Self Consciousness

Ryan Tufts · December 18, 2021

So it may be the very self consciousness that inspires art also hides it. My desire to share the meaning of life’s experience with you is too intense to be denied though. Please forgive my shortcomings and please point out my inconsistencies. I hope that this website adds something beautiful to the shared human experience and this wonderous journey into the unknown beyond. And the One who brought us here will take us back again.

Today I posted the Meaning Project www.sgttomas.com/meaning. This is a labour of love and of necessity that I have been working on for almost two decades. Glory be to God in the highest, above what I associate with Him. We don’t have a direct experience of God in this life, but there are signs and His Names bring forth existence with the mere “be” and it is. This project is a search for the meaning of the Name of the LORD. It unites all seekers on the Journey Home. We have unity in the Name of the LORD and the bond and grace we receive from our time with each other is precious.

Peace,

~sgttomas
